05 — Negotiation Levers
The Four Levers Nexus Will Push
Each lever has a specific ask, a specific carrier, and a specific dollar impact. Nothing is hopeful.
1
Network Re-Price Request
Push the TPA / network for a re-price on inpatient and outpatient facility contracts at our top 3 utilized hospitals. Reference cost data is on the table.
Expected Win
3–7% off facility line items
2
Stop-Loss RFP — 3 Carriers
Take spec + agg to incumbent plus two competitive markets. Goal: hold the spec increase under 15% and refuse any laser without a credible release schedule.
Expected Win
10–25% under incumbent's first quote
3
Plan Design Tweaks
Move imaging to a Center of Excellence tier, add a GLP-1 step-therapy edit, expand telehealth $0 copay. Steers utilization without cost-shifting to employees.
Expected Win
$80K–$140K Y1 utilization shift
4
Carrier Performance Scorecard
Ground the renewal ask in measurable carrier failures: turnaround time on prior auth, payment accuracy, network adequacy. Numbers, not opinions.
Expected Win
Service credits + posture for next year
Stacked outcome:
If 3 of 4 land at the midpoint, we offset roughly $220K–$340K of the projected renewal increase. That's the ask we walk in with.
