01 — The Problem
The Annual Renewal Has Become a Tax
7–10%
Average annual group health premium increase
Five consecutive years. Compounded, that's a ~50% premium increase over five years for the same plan — often without meaningful benefit improvements. The renewal isn't a market event anymore. It's a recurring tax on payroll.
Source: KFF Employer Health Benefits Survey 2020–2024 (national avg. 6.5–7.2% range, with mid-market trending higher).
The Limits of Control
Plan design tweaks, deductible nudges, carrier shopping — these are deck-chair moves. The underlying risk pool, the network economics, the trend rate — none of it is yours to change.
What Employers Actually Want
Predictable budget. A line item that doesn't surprise the CFO every September. ICHRA is one of the few structures that delivers that.
What ICHRA Is
An Individual Coverage Health Reimbursement Arrangement — IRS-compliant since 2020. Employers fund a tax-free monthly allowance. Employees buy their own ACA plan. Reimbursement runs through payroll.
02 — What It Is
A Defined-Contribution Approach to Health Benefits
Instead of buying a group plan, the employer funds a tax-free monthly allowance. Employees use it to buy their own individual ACA plan that fits them. Reimbursement is administered through payroll.
Step 1
Employer Funds an Allowance
A defined monthly dollar amount per employee — tax-free for the employer, tax-free for the employee.
→
Step 2
Employee Buys an Individual Plan
On the ACA marketplace or off-exchange. Their plan, their carrier, their network.
→
Step 3
Reimbursement via Payroll
Employee submits proof of coverage. Employer reimburses up to the allowance — tax-free, on-cycle.

The Class-Size Rule
If you offer a group plan to some employees and ICHRA to others, IRS minimum class sizes apply (10 / 20 / 30 employees depending on total headcount). Modeling matters here.
Affordability for ALEs
For employers with 50+ FTEs, the allowance must be high enough that the lowest-cost Silver plan minus the allowance falls within ACA affordability (9.02% of household income, 2025). We model this for every class.
Age-Banding Allowed
Because individual market rates rise with age, the allowance can scale up to a 3:1 ratio (oldest:youngest) within a class — which often makes ICHRA more equitable than a flat group premium share.
03 — Decision Framework
Who ICHRA Works For
ICHRA is a fit for specific employer profiles. If three or four of these describe you, this is a real conversation.
Distributed Workforce
Employees scattered across states or rating regions. One group plan can't be optimal for everyone — individual plans are localized by definition.
Varied Employee Needs
Mix of young/single, family-stage, near-retirement. Each picks the plan that actually fits — HSA-friendly bronze, rich silver, or HMO with a tight network.
Wanting Fixed Benefit Cost
CFO is tired of 8% renewals. ICHRA converts a variable, claims-driven cost into a defined-contribution line item that you control year over year.
Strong Individual Markets
States with deep ACA marketplaces and competitive carriers — Michigan included. Strong individual markets mean employees actually have plans to choose from.
03 — Honest Check
Who It Doesn't Work For
ICHRA is a decision framework, not a panacea. If any of these describe you, the math probably doesn't pencil out — and we'll tell you that.
01
Fewer than 10 employees
Below ~10 employees, the administrative overhead of running ICHRA (platform fees, payroll setup, compliance) starts to outweigh the savings. A traditional small-group plan or association health plan often makes more sense at that scale.
02
High-claimants in the existing pool
If your group is currently rated favorably because of underwriting (level-funded with great experience, for example), moving to ICHRA shifts those individuals into community-rated individual plans — and you give up the underwriting advantage. Run the comparison both ways before deciding.
03
Employees uncomfortable shopping ACA
ICHRA shifts plan-selection decisions to the employee. That's a feature for some workforces and a friction point for others. If your team expects HR to hand them a card on day one and never think about it again, expect higher support load — or pick a different structure.

Read the trade-off clearly: ICHRA shifts predictability to the employer and plan selection + annual rate exposure to the employee. That's the deal. If you want the employer to absorb claims volatility, stay with group. If you want a fixed line item and your team can shop, ICHRA wins.
