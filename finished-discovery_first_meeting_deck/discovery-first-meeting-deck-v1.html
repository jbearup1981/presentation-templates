Same advisor, same account manager, same processor — every call. You won't be re-explaining your group every time someone leaves.

05 — Document handoff
What we need from you
Five items get us from this meeting to a real proposal. The first three are usually a five-minute email to your current broker. The last two we'll send and walk you through.
Latest medical invoice
Most recent monthly billing statement from your medical carrier — confirms current rates, tier mix, and headcount on the plan.
Current census
DOB, gender, ZIP, coverage tier, dependents — anonymized is fine. We have a template if you don't have one ready.
Plan documents (current SBC + benefit summaries)
Medical, dental, vision, life — whatever's in force. So we can quote apples to apples.
Claims experience report (if level- or self-funded)
12-24 months of paid claims, large claimants, and stop-loss reports. Without it, no underwriter will give us a real number.
Broker of Record letter (signed)
One-page disclosure that lets us pull data and quote on your behalf. Doesn't bind you to anything — fully reversible until you accept a proposal.
