02 — The Difference
How we're different from your current broker
Most brokers show up at renewal, swap a carrier, and disappear. That's not a service model — that's a sales model. Here's how we operate.
A Named 3-Person Service Pod
Lead advisor, account manager, processor — you know their names and cell numbers. No 1-800 line. No ticket queue. No "let me transfer you." When someone calls in, they hit a person who already knows your group.
Direct Carrier Relationships
We talk to underwriters, account execs, and claims teams directly — not call centers. When something needs to move, we know who to call and they pick up. Your renewal isn't sitting in a queue with 400 other accounts.
Quarterly Claims Reviews
Every three months we sit down with your claims data and tell you what's coming. Trends, large claimants, utilization patterns — the stuff that drives next year's renewal. No surprises 60 days out.
Compliance Auditing — Included
5500s, ACA reporting, Section 125 plan documents, Wrap SPDs, COBRA notices, DOL audit prep. Most brokers charge extra. We don't — it's table stakes. If a DOL letter shows up, you forward it to us.

06 — Honest Disclosure
What we're not
We'd rather tell you up front who we're not built for than waste a meeting. If any of these are deal-breakers for you, we should both know now.
We're not national
We're a West Michigan firm. We work in your zip code, we drive to your office, we know your local carriers and TPAs personally. If you have offices in 12 states and need a national broker network, we're the wrong firm. Be honest about that.
We're not for 500+ lives
Our sweet spot is 25–250 lives. Once you cross 500, you need a different funding strategy, a different compliance model, and frankly a different broker. We'll tell you when you've outgrown us — and we'll help with the handoff.
We're not your HR outsource
We're a benefits broker. We can hand you a great PEO, payroll, or HRIS partner — but we're not trying to be one. We do benefits, compliance, and claims strategy. We do those things very well. We don't try to do everything.
07 — The Discovery Meeting
Bring four things
If you say yes to a 30-minute discovery call, here's what to have on hand. None of it is hard to find. We've made the meeting useful for you whether or not we end up working together.
1
Your Last Carrier Invoice
Most recent monthly billing statement from your medical carrier. Tells us tier counts, current rates, and admin fees.
2
Current Census
Headcount with DOB, zip, gender, coverage tier. A simple Excel export from payroll works fine.
3
Claims Report (if you have it)
Last 12–24 months of claims data from the carrier. If your group is over 50, ask for it — you're entitled. Skip if fully insured under 50.
4
Your Top 3 Gripes
What's driving you crazy about your current setup? Cost, service, communication, claims, comp­li­ance? Tell us straight. We work backward from there.
