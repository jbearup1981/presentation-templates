Designed to **complement existing major medical — not replace it.** Also works for groups with no group medical at all.

## The Four-Step Structure

1. **[[Amaze_Health|Amaze Health]]** — 24/7/365 virtual primary, urgent, and mental health care for employee + entire household. The clinical engine.
2. **Pre-Tax Payroll Deduction** — premiums deducted pre-tax, reducing taxable income for employee and employer.
3. **Comprehensive Benefit Package** — accident indemnity, prescription savings, biological screening.
4. **Wellbeing Claim Payment** — monthly claims payment to the employee that offsets the premium, often netting a take-home increase.

## What Every Participant Receives

- **CARE** — Unlimited [[Amaze_Health|Amaze Health]]: primary, urgent, mental health, chronic condition mgmt, specialty consults, care coordination, medical bill advocacy. 150+ providers, 80+ languages, response <30 seconds. **Entire household including pets.**
- **PROTECTION** — Fixed indemnity policy paying **cash benefits directly to the employee, tax-free** for hospitalization, ICU, emergency treatment, ambulance, fractures, lacerations, burns.
- **WELLNESS** — 1,000+ commonly prescribed medications at no charge plus discounts. Mental health + chronic condition support. Advocacy: referrals, prior auths, billing questions, navigation.
- **THE MATH** — Pre-tax deduction lowers taxable income; combined with monthly claims payments, most participants see a small net increase in take-home.

## Implementation — 8 Weeks, Ending in Passive Enrollment

| Step | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Week 1 | **Setup & Documentation** — plan docs, payroll codes, §125 paperwork; short session with payroll lead |
| 2 | Weeks 1–2 | **Communication Plan** — manager briefing, webinar cadence, employee email + SMS plan queued |
| 3 | Weeks 2–7 | **Education & Passive Enrollment** — 3–6 weeks of webinars, emails, personalized paycheck previews. No 1-on-1s required; one-click opt-out |
| 4 | Week 8 | **Go Live** — first payroll deduction; Nexus monitors first cycles |

**Ongoing every pay cycle — Automated Payroll Monitoring:** software pulls a payroll report each run, recalculates every enrolled employee's tax position, flags anyone whose situation changed. Negatively-impacted employees are moved to a different tier or disenrolled, communicated to payroll before finalization. *"You don't manage exceptions. We do."*

**Materials Nexus provides:** manager education kit · employee webinar series · email + SMS cadence · personalized paycheck preview · new-hire onboarding flow · one-click opt-out.

Elsewhere the site says "most groups go from kickoff to fully operational in **30–45 days**" — softer than the 8-week timeline. Minor inconsistency.

## Key Compliance / Objection-Handling Content

- **Three pillars:** §125 Cafeteria Plan (in code since 1978) · HIPAA **excepted benefits** / fixed indemnity · IRC §106 / §105.
- **Delivered in writing at implementation:** plan document, SPD, required ACA/ERISA notices, carrier's outside-ERISA-counsel opinion letter, IRS private letter ruling history.
- **Not subject to [[cobra-notice-requirements-and-penalties|COBRA]]** — structured as an excepted benefit.
- **Monthly engagement requirement** — carrier needs documentation of a qualifying health interaction each month for the claim payment to issue. Satisfied by a telehealth visit, a ~2-minute wellness check-in, or any covered service use. Miss a month → no indemnity payment that month, no penalty, no clawback, still enrolled.
- **Passive enrollment** — auto-enrolled, opt-out window roughly 4 weeks before and 4 weeks after. Compared to 401(k) auto-enrollment (30% → 80%+ participation).
- **Expected participation:** 70–90% sustained (vs <20% typical voluntary benefits). Explicitly *not* guaranteed.
- **Benefit payment taxability:** may be taxable as **income** (not wages, no FICA) if benefits exceed unreimbursed §213(d) medical expenses. Carrier handles reporting. IRS tried to change treatment in 2023 (CCA 202323006 + proposed reg); **neither finalized** — 1969 rule governs.
- **Social Security impact — disclosed honestly:** ~$13.71/mo reduction at full retirement age per year of enrollment (on an $18,000/yr wage reduction through the 32% bend point).

| Years enrolled | Monthly SSA reduction | Annual |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | ~$69/mo | ~$823/yr |
| 10 | ~$137/mo | ~$1,646/yr |
| 15 | ~$206/mo | ~$2,468/yr |
| 20 | ~$274/mo | ~$3,289/yr |
| 30 | ~$411/mo | ~$4,937/yr |

- **401(k) redirect play** — $27/paycheck at 8% → ~$79K over 30 yrs (~$118K with 50% match); at 4% withdrawal ≈ $4,720/yr, nearly offsetting the $4,937/yr SSA reduction. $50/paycheck → ~$236K with match, ~$9,440/yr, well ahead.
- **Termination:** deduction stops with final check, no clawback, no [[cobra-notice-requirements-and-penalties|COBRA]]. Departing employees may continue Amaze individually — **$49/mo individual, $59/mo family.**
