What Nexus Health Plus is
A pretax wrap that pairs a fixed-indemnity supplemental policy with 24/7 virtual primary care — sitting alongside the existing group medical plan, not replacing it.
01 — The Anatomy
Four parts. One wrap. One paycheck line.
1
Pretax Engine
Section 125 Pretax Contribution
Employee elects a pretax payroll deduction inside an ERISA cafeteria plan. The dollar drops out of the FICA & income-tax base for both sides on day one.
2
Fixed Indemnity
Supplemental Indemnity Policy
A fully insured group fixed-indemnity policy filed in Michigan. Pays a scheduled wellbeing benefit back to the employee — the carrier holds all insurance risk.
3
Care Backbone
24/7 Virtual Primary Care
Amaze Health full-time providers. Diagnose, treat, prescribe, refer. $0 copay for the employee and the whole household. Mental health and chronic-care included.
4
Engagement
Wellness & Education Credits
A brief monthly wellbeing activity unlocks the indemnity benefit. Drives engagement with the Amaze platform and keeps the program clean under §213(d).
Wraps around — not replaces — existing benefits. The group medical plan, dental, vision, and 401(k) keep doing what they do. NHP plugs into the empty space those plans don't fill.

04 — The Fit
Where Nexus Health Plus belongs
✓
25+ W-2 Employees
The math compounds with headcount. At 25 enrolled lives, the program is meaningful; at 100+, it's transformative. Mix of hourly and salaried is fine.
✓
Stable Workforce
Annualized turnover under 35%. NHP rewards employees who stay enrolled across pay periods — the value compounds, not the burnout.
✓
Existing Group Medical Plan
NHP is supplemental, not a replacement. It assumes the underlying group medical (or ICHRA) is already in place. We coexist with BCBS, Priority Health, and every major MI carrier.
✓
Boost Take-Home Without a Wage Hike
CFOs that need a retention tool but don't have room for a 3% comp adjustment. NHP delivers ~$700/yr more take-home per enrolled employee at zero net employer cost.
04 — The Honest Read
Where it doesn't belong
×
Independent Contractor Workforce
NHP runs through W-2 payroll. 1099 contractors aren't on the cafeteria plan and can't access the §125 deduction. If your headcount is mostly contractors, this isn't the lever.
×
High-Turnover Operations
Annualized turnover above ~50%. Re-enrolling and re-disclosing every 90 days creates more admin friction than the FICA savings can offset.
×
Already HSA-Heavy Strategy
If your population is fully maxing HSA contributions paired with an HDHP, the pretax buckets are already crowded. We can model a partial overlay, but the lift is smaller.
×
Maxed Pretax Elections
Employees already capped on 401(k) + FSA + dependent care don't have payroll headroom for a meaningful NHP election. We screen for this in the design session before anyone signs anything.
If NHP isn't a fit, we'll tell you in the first meeting. Saying no to a bad-fit deployment is part of how we keep the program clean for the employers it serves well.
04 — Compliance
Built on settled law. Wrapped in real disclosure.
✓
Plan Architecture
ERISA-Compliant Wrap
Cafeteria plan document, summary plan description, wrap document, and required Form 5500 filings. Insight Benefits maintains the entire compliance file — you get the binder, not the homework.
✓
Tax Treatment
Section 125 PLR-Backed Structure
Pretax payroll deduction inside a §125 cafeteria plan. Indemnity benefits paid as income (not wages). Backed by Rev. Rul. 69-154 and the §213(d) excess-benefit rule — settled law for decades.
✓
Coexistence
Supplemental, Not a Replacement
NHP is a separate fixed-indemnity policy with no coordination of benefits against the group medical plan. Excepted-benefit conditions under 45 CFR §148.220 satisfied. Group medical and ACA reporting are unaffected.
Voluntary enrollment, transparent disclosure. Every employee receives a written summary of how the indemnity benefit is taxed and the long-run Social Security impact of pretax wage reduction. Nobody is forced in. If it doesn't pencil for an employee, they don't enroll.
